Chapter 02 · Compensation

CASEY understands your paycheck.

Live Gusto pay meets the real Safeshield comp plan, Deal Log production, accruing commission, and closed-period history. CASEY can explain what happened and calculate what comes next.

How the answer is built

Your numbers. Your rules. Your next move.

CASEY does not guess. It joins the authoritative sources, scopes the answer to the person asking, and uses the exact compensation engine.

Open it from CASEY’s left navigation for the next paycheck, recent checks, commission lines, year-to-date earnings, accruing commission, scheduled payouts, and the red flag when a check finalizes.

Direct-message CASEY in Google Chat when you want an explanation, goal plan, pace check, or a concrete next-action calculation.

Give CASEY agency commission revenue, or provide written premium together with the carrier rate. It will not invent missing economics.

What would I make on $1,000 of agency commission revenue?
If I write three more average deals, where does that put me?
Private by design: pay and salary work only in a private 1:1 chat. CASEY verifies the employee and has no selector for asking about somebody else.

Create it. Sign it. File it.
