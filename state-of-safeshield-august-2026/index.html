The next version of Casey

What is new

These are the high-level changes. Casey now holds the complete interactive explanation, practical examples, and demos, so you can learn each workflow before you need it with a real client.

01

Casey is now client-facing

Casey can guide a prospect, adapt the intake, collect useful files, and preserve the story for the producer.Agent example: You begin the first call with the prospect's needs and documents instead of only a name and phone number.

02

Gusto and private pay coaching are now in Casey

Ask private questions about your own paycheck, salary, commissions, year-to-date earnings, or goal planning.Agent example: Ask what weekly production pace could move you toward a specific earnings goal.

03

New SEO opportunities feed one intelligent intake

Calls, ads, website activity, Google Chat, and new search opportunities can move through one core intake that adapts across thirteen industries.Agent example: A restaurant lead arrives with its source, industry-specific answers, files, and next step already together.

04

Safeshield Secure is our document-signing standard

Use protected uploads, Smart Sign, signer order, reusable links, progress tracking, completed PDFs, and retained audit evidence. Do not search for another signing provider.Agent example: If a client cannot find the automated email, place the same protected signing link in your own follow-up or welcome email without rebuilding the package.

05

Plymouth Rock API access is approved

This is the approved path toward a connected online home quote and bind experience. It is coming soon and is not live yet.Agent example: The future flow can move one guided homeowner intake toward the carrier connection without rebuilding the household.

06

Casey now has an Update Center

Important releases can appear as one short sign-in story and remain under the notification bell. Every update includes what changed, why it matters, and a practical agent example.Your part: Try new workflows before you urgently need them and tell us what is unclear, missing, or broken.
