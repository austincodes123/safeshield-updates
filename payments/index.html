📏 Rules of the road

  • Never send a client anything marked UAT or TEST. Production cards say PRODUCTION in green.
  • Always collect through the card. It's our system of record — every link, fee, and payment is logged.
  • Every broker fee gets the disclosure form signed — download it from the card, RabbitSign it, file it. Fees are always itemized to the client and never exceed 11% of premium.
  • The client always sees exactly what they're paying and why. Casey never sends anything to a client without you clicking send.
